# Analyst Memo — Shopify App Store Insights

**Dashboard:** Shopify App Store Analysis
**Reporting Period:** 2018–202

**Key Insight**

Majority of reviews fall within 4–5 stars, suggesting overall positive perception. There was also. a small portion of 1–2 star reviews indicates areas for possible improvement.

**Business Impact**

Most users are happy, suggesting strong retention potential. However, if the small group of 1–2 star reviews shares a common complaint, it signals an unfixed issue that could eventually spread and affect satisfaction among the currently happy majority.

**Recommendation**

An assigned team should investigate the 1–2 star reviews to identify if there is a similar complaint (or recurring pattern) across them.
